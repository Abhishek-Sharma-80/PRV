### 1. ZED CERTIFICATION
What is ZED?
ZED means Zero Defect Zero Effect. It is an initiative introduced for Indian MSMEs to improve quality, productivity, manufacturing systems, environmental responsibility, and competitiveness. The objective is to help organizations manufacture high-quality products while reducing environmental impact.
Suitable For: MSMEs, Manufacturing Companies, Engineering Units, Component Manufacturers, Export Companies, Startups involved in manufacturing.
Business Benefits: Improves Quality, Reduces Rejection, Improves Productivity, Better Process Control, Customer Confidence, Competitive Advantage, Supports continuous improvement, May improve eligibility for certain government initiatives where applicable.
General Implementation Process: Business Assessment → Gap Analysis → Documentation → Implementation → Training → Internal Review → Assessment → Certification (subject to successful assessment).
Common Documents: Company Registration, GST, PAN, Organization Details, Process Documents, Quality Records, Production Records, Training Records.

### 2. ISO 9001
What is ISO 9001?
ISO 9001 is an internationally recognized Quality Management System (QMS) standard that helps organizations establish consistent processes to improve product and service quality, customer satisfaction, and continual improvement.
Suitable For: Manufacturing, IT, Education, Healthcare, Construction, Engineering, Service Sector, Government Organizations, MSMEs.
Business Benefits: Improved customer satisfaction, Standardized processes, Better documentation, Reduced errors, Improved efficiency, Improved credibility, Supports continual improvement.
General Implementation Process: Gap Analysis → Planning → Documentation → Implementation → Training → Internal Audit → Management Review → Certification Audit.
Typical Documentation: Quality Policy, Quality Objectives, Process Documentation, SOPs, Risk Register, Internal Audit Records, Management Review Records, Training Records, Corrective Action Records.
Common Questions: What is ISO 9001?, Who needs it?, What are the benefits?, How long does implementation usually take?, What documents are generally required?, How does certification work?, How can PRV assist?
PRV Support: Gap Assessment, Documentation Guidance, Employee Awareness, Implementation Support, Internal Audit Assistance, Certification Readiness.
