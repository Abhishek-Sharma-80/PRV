Knowledge Base

Resources, Guides & Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to understand standards, audit preparation, subsidies, and implementation timelines.

What is the government subsidy available under MSME ZED Scheme?

Micro enterprises receive 80%, Small enterprises receive 60%, and Medium enterprises receive 50% subsidy on certification costs, ₹2 Lakhs consulting handholding support grant, and up to ₹3 Lakhs for technology upgradation & capital testing equipment acquisition.

How long does it take to achieve ISO 9001:2015 certification?

For small and medium enterprises, our fast-track process typically takes between 15 to 25 days, including documentation, SOP drafting, internal auditor training, and Stage 1 / Stage 2 audits.

What is the NATS / NAPS Apprenticeship stipend reimbursement amount?

Under NAPS (National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme), the Government reimburses 25% of prescribed stipend up to a maximum of ₹1,500 per apprentice per month directly through DBT.
