Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to standard client queries regarding certifications, audits, subsidies, and implementation timelines.

ISO 9001:2015 is the premier international standard for Quality Management Systems (QMS). It establishes standard operating procedures (SOPs), customer satisfaction frameworks, and continuous risk-based quality assurance. It is mandatory for participating in government tenders, OEM vendor onboarding, corporate vendor registrations, and global export client eligibility.
Zero Defect Zero Effect (ZED) is a flagship scheme by the Ministry of MSME, Govt of India, to rate manufacturers on Bronze, Silver, and Gold standards. MSMEs receive up to 80% reimbursement on certification cost, up to ₹5 Lakh handholding support, 0.5% bank loan interest subsidy, and ₹1 Lakh subsidy for testing equipment.
IATF 16949:2016 is the global automotive industry quality management standard aligned with ISO 9001:2015. It is mandatory for Tier-1, Tier-2, and Tier-3 suppliers delivering components to OEMs like Maruti Suzuki, Tata Motors, Hyundai, Hero, Mahindra, etc. It mandates 5 Core Tools: APQP, PPAP, FMEA, MSA, and SPC.
SMETA (SEDEX Members Ethical Trade Audit) evaluates factory working conditions across 2 Pillars (Labor Standards + Health & Safety) or 4 Pillars (adds Environmental Assessment + Business Ethics). International buyers in Europe & US require SMETA audit clearance before issuing purchase orders to textile, food, and manufacturing exporters.
FSSAI (Food Safety & Standards Authority of India) license is legally required for food processing units, hotels, catering, and food traders. PRV Consultancy provides end-to-end FSSAI state/central licensing, FOSTAC food supervisor training, hygiene audit readiness, and Schedule 4 sanitary implementation.
Under NATS & NAPS schemes, factories and corporate companies can onboard apprentices while claiming up to ₹1,500/month per candidate stipend reimbursement from the Government of India. PRV handles portal empanelment, apprentice registration, contract approval, and monthly stipend claim submissions.
