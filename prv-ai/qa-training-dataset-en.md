## 1. ISO 9001

### Q1. What is ISO 9001?
**Answer:** ISO 9001 is an international standard for a Quality Management System (QMS). It provides a structured framework for managing processes, meeting customer requirements, improving consistency, and supporting continual improvement.

### Q2. Why does a company need ISO 9001?
**Answer:** Organizations may implement ISO 9001 to strengthen their quality management system, improve process control, increase customer confidence, reduce inconsistencies, and establish a structured approach to continual improvement.

### Q3. Which companies can implement ISO 9001?
**Answer:** ISO 9001 can be applicable to organizations across many sectors, including manufacturing, engineering, IT, healthcare, education, construction, trading, and services. Exact applicability depends on the organization's activities and scope.

### Q4. What does ISO 9001 cover?
**Answer:** ISO 9001 addresses areas such as organizational context, leadership, planning, support, operations, performance evaluation, and improvement within a quality management system.

### Q5. What documents are required for ISO 9001?
**Answer:** The required documented information depends on the organization's scope and processes. Depending on the organization, it may include policies, objectives, process information, operational records, monitoring records, audit records, and corrective-action records.

### Q6. What is the ISO 9001 implementation process?
**Answer:** A typical implementation approach may include current-system assessment, gap analysis, planning, documentation, implementation, employee awareness and training, internal review, corrective actions, and preparation for the certification audit.

### Q7. What is the difference between ISO 9001 and ISO 14001?
**Answer:** ISO 9001 focuses on quality management, while ISO 14001 focuses on environmental management. An organization may implement one or multiple management systems depending on its business requirements.

### Q8. Is ISO 9001 mandatory?
**Answer:** ISO 9001 may be required or requested depending on the organization's industry, customers, contracts, tenders, or other applicable requirements. The specific business context should be checked before determining whether it is mandatory.

### Q9. How can PRV help with ISO 9001?
**Answer:** PRV can support organizations with assessment, gap identification, implementation planning, documentation guidance, training, process improvement, and audit-readiness support.

### Q4. What areas are considered under ZED?

### Q5. What is the difference between ZED and ISO 9001?
**Answer:** ISO 9001 primarily focuses on a Quality Management System, while ZED has a broader manufacturing-excellence focus that can include quality, productivity, waste reduction, and environmental considerations.

### Q6. Can a company have both ISO 9001 and ZED?
**Answer:** Yes. An organization can adopt multiple improvement and management-system frameworks according to its business needs. ISO 9001 and ZED have different focuses and can complement each other.

## CROSS-SERVICE CUSTOMER QUESTIONS

### Q1. Which certification is best for my company?
**Answer:** There is no single certification that is best for every company. The right option depends on your industry, products or services, company size, customer requirements, current systems, and business objectives. Please share your industry and main objective so I can narrow down the relevant options.

### Q2. Which is better: ISO 9001, ZED, or IATF 16949?
**Answer:** They have different purposes. ISO 9001 focuses on general quality management, ZED has a broader manufacturing-excellence focus, and IATF 16949 addresses automotive-sector quality requirements. The most suitable option depends on your organization's industry and requirements.

### Q3. I need an ISO quotation. What information do you need?
**Answer:** Quotation requirements can depend on factors such as organization size, scope, number of locations, employee strength, existing systems, and the level of consulting support required. These details should be collected before providing a quotation.

### Q4. How many days will certification take?
**Answer:** A fixed timeline should not be promised. The timeline can depend on organizational readiness, scope, documentation, implementation requirements, audit scheduling, and other project-specific factors.

### Q5. Does PRV guarantee certification?
**Answer:** PRV can provide consultancy, implementation, training, and audit-readiness support, but certification approval should not be guaranteed. The final certification outcome depends on the applicable independent certification or assessment process.

### Q6. My company has a quality problem. What should I do?
**Answer:** The first step is to understand the actual problem and identify its root causes. Areas such as defects, rework, customer complaints, process variation, downtime, and process controls can then be assessed to determine the appropriate improvement approach.

### Q7. I only want the certificate. I don't want implementation.
**Answer:** A management-system certificate has greater practical value when the system is actually implemented within the organization's processes. PRV should focus on practical implementation and readiness rather than promising a certificate without proper system development.
