## 2. CERTIFICATIONS MATRIX

### ZED (Zero Effect Zero Defect)
* **Category**: Manufacturing / MSME-focused business excellence and quality/productivity improvement framework.
* **Definition**: ZED ka objective organizations ko better quality, improved productivity, reduced defects and improved environmental performance ki direction me systematically improve karne me help karna hai.
* **Target Audience**: Manufacturing MSMEs with valid Udyam Registration.
* **Benefits**: Process discipline, quality improvement, defect reduction, productivity improvement, waste reduction, continuous improvement culture.
* **ZED vs ISO 9001**: ZED focuses broadly on manufacturing waste/productivity/environment for MSMEs, while ISO 9001 is a formal quality management system standard applicable across all sectors. Neither automatically replaces the other.

### ISO 9001 (Quality Management System)
* **Definition**: ISO 9001 is an internationally recognized standard for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually improving a Quality Management System (QMS).
* **Purpose**: Consistent quality, process control, customer satisfaction, risk-based thinking, continual improvement.
* **Applicability**: Manufacturing, Service, IT, Education, Healthcare, Engineering, Construction, Trading, Logistics.
* **Key Process**: Initial Assessment → Gap Analysis → QMS Planning → Process Identification → Documentation → Training → Implementation → Internal Audit → Management Review → Corrective Actions → Certification Audit.

### ISO 14001 (Environmental Management System)
* **Purpose**: Organization ko environmental aspects and impacts ko systematically manage karne me help karta hai.
* **Areas**: Waste management, resource efficiency, legal/environmental compliance, pollution prevention.
* **Target**: Manufacturing, chemical, textile, construction, food, pharmaceutical.

### ISO 45001 (Occupational Health & Safety)
* **Purpose**: Workplace health and safety risks ko systematically manage karna.
* **Areas**: Hazard identification (HIRA), risk assessment, operational controls, worker participation, emergency preparedness.

### ISO 22000 (Food Safety Management System)
* **Focus**: Food safety management throughout food-chain processes using HACCP principles.
* **Rule**: ISO 22000 (international quality standard) is NOT the same as FSSAI (Indian statutory legal licensing).

### ISO 27001 (Information Security Management System)
* **Purpose**: Information security risks, asset protection, access control, and GDPR/data protection management for IT/SaaS/FinTech.

### ISO 22301 (Business Continuity Management System)
* **Purpose**: Organizational resilience and continuation of critical business activities during disruptions/disasters.

### ISO 50001 (Energy Management System)
* **Purpose**: Systematic energy review, efficiency improvement, and power/fuel cost reduction for energy-intensive plants.

### ISO 13485 (Medical Devices QMS)
* **Focus**: Quality management across medical device design, manufacturing, and CDSCO/CE regulatory compliance.

### ISO 17025 (Testing & Calibration Laboratories)
* **Purpose**: Technical competence and measurement accuracy for testing and calibration laboratories (NABL context).

### IATF 16949 (Automotive Quality Management)
* **Focus**: Defect prevention and reduction of variation in automotive supply chains.
* **Recommendation Logic**: If Industry = Automobile + Product = Automotive Components + Supply = OEM, consider IATF 16949 relevance after ISO 9001 baseline. Do not state mandatory without verifying customer demand.

## 3. AUDITS & COMPLIANCE

* **SEDEX**: Supply-chain data-sharing platform for responsible sourcing. Never refer to SEDEX as an ISO certification.
* **SMETA**: Sedex Members Ethical Trade Audit methodology (Labor standards, Health & Safety, Environment, Business Ethics).
* **Social Audit**: Workplace and social compliance evaluation based on buyer code of conduct and labor laws.
* **MACE Audit**: Client-specific supply chain audit requirement handled by analyzing specific customer requirement documents.
* **FSSAI**: Food Safety and Standards Authority of India mandatory statutory licensing/registration for Indian food businesses.

* **5S**: Workplace organization methodology (Sort, Set in Order, Shine, Standardize, Sustain). Reduces searching time, clutter, and accidents.
* **Lean Manufacturing**: Maximizing customer value while eliminating 8 Wastes (Defects, Overproduction, Waiting, Non-utilized talent, Transportation, Inventory, Motion, Extra-processing).
* **Kaizen**: Continuous improvement through small, systematic shopfloor changes.
* **Quality Improvement**: Systematic root-cause analysis (RCA), defect reduction, and standardization.
* **Productivity Improvement**: Optimizing machine utilization, process flow, setup time, and bottleneck elimination.
* **Cost Reduction**: Waste reduction and efficiency optimization (never immediate layoffs or aggressive gutting).
* **Operational Excellence**: End-to-end integration of quality, speed, safety, and cost management.
